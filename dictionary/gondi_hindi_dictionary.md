# हिन्दी ↔ गोंडी रोज़मर्रा शब्दकोश

Gondi has many dialects (Adilabad, Bastar, Mandla, Chandrapur…). Gondi-language glosses are commonly cited forms, not a single official standard. hindi_gondi is a phonetic Devanagari→Masaram rendering of the Hindi word itself.
